# Segment A visual QA (Clawd on paper)

Scope: t 0–7.45 and 28.75–31.92. Measured on ref/f frames vs fresh renders (grain off). Coordinates are 1920x1080 unless marked (480) = 480x270 analysis scale. Frame k (0-based) = t·24; ref file = k+1.
Overall: zoom curve, Clawd size/position and eye placement match closely. Main problems are cadence (smoothness), one blank frame, paper colour, ground line, and hatch style.

1. **Cadence is wrong in the paper parts (smoothness, top priority).** t 0.58–6.3 and 29.8–31.92. Reference is strictly on twos: content changes only on even frames k, and holds on odd k (hold runs = 1 everywhere). Ours mixes hold runs of 1 and 2 frames, and during the walk-in it steps on a 3-frame beat (moves at k=24,27,28,30,33,34,36,39,40,…). In 0–7.45 that is 52 frames with the wrong hold/move state; in 28.8–31.9, 16 frames. Fix: quantise the time used for Clawd, his bob/legs, and the paper linework: `t_eff = 2*floor(k/2)/24` for 0–6.3 and 29.8–31.92. Check that the hold runs are exactly 1 afterwards.
2. **Walk-in stutters because of the cadence.** Example: k=32 (1.333 s) ref cx=67.0 (480), ours 55.1, then ours jumps to 66.9 at k=33. Steps land on the same positions as the reference, just on the wrong frames, so the fix in item 1 should remove the stutter. Verify with the cx column of out/qa_Ac/seq.txt (deleted, re-create with the seq.py method).
3. **Blank frame at 29.833 (k=716).** Ours draws empty paper with only the ground line. Ref shows Clawd (about 5138 orange px at 480 scale; body centre ~(240,159) at 480). Ours is a 1-frame pop to nothing. Find the time window or cut handover that clears the scene at 29.83 and keep Clawd drawn through it.
4. **Paper colour and vignette.** Ref sampled: TL #E6E6DE, TR #E9E9E0, BR #E6E6DE, edge-right #E6E6DE, centre-top #EAEAE1, texture std ~3. Ours: corners #FFFFFE, TR #F9FBF5, BR #F9FCF6, edge-right #FBFEF8, centre #EAEAE3, almost flat (std <1). Fix: remove the white radial glow; base paper ~#E8E8E0 with a darkening falloff of about −8 levels at the corners; add ±3 level mottling.
5. **Ground line.** Ref: 1 px at y=804 (0.744 H), colour about #9D9D94 (value 157 on bg 228), x from 124 to 1861. Ours: y=803 with a 2 px soft halo, value 173 on bg 243, x from 146 to 1767. Fix: y=804, x 124–1861, hard 1 px line, colour #9D9D94, no halo.
6. **Body hatching is the wrong texture.** Ref body fill #D87754 is flat (std 1.5) with diagonal darker pencil strokes at about −60°, crosshatched near the edges. Ours fill #DB7955 has std ~10 from horizontal light dashes. Visible at 4.8 s and 29.3 s. Fix: switch the hatch to ~−60° darker strokes (about #B85E40 at 40% alpha), remove or reduce the light horizontal streaks, and keep the body fill at #D87754.
7. **Soup-bubble lead-in 28.5–28.8 (segment E owner, but it feeds the return).** At k=684–689 ours bubble is offset to the right and does not show the reference's rainbow Mandelbrot edge bands or the same colour blobs at the same scale. Compare ref/f/0685–0690 with out/qa_Ac/seq3. Fix: match the bubble centre and scale of the reference at those frames, and check K.soup() call args for segment E.
8. **Flash-in at 28.833 (k=692).** Ref is the soup frame at the same scale as ours (eye window 145–330 px at 480 for both), but the centroid offset at 480 is 37 px (ours cx 276 vs ref 239), so ours shows the window shifted right for one frame. Fix: centre the soup window at (240,135) at 480 (≈ (960,540) at 1920) on the first soup frame.
9. **Pencil spiral flash frames 28.75 and 28.79.** Spiral geometry matches. Ref strokes are heavier (~3 px at 1920) and darker (#2A211D), and the eye oval is larger. Ours strokes are thinner and greyer. Fix: stroke weight 3 px, colour #2A211D, eye ellipse about 1.3× bigger.
10. **Pull-out 29.08–29.33 runs ~0.3–0.5 frame early.** Ref cx 247.9 (k=698) vs ours 250.3; 260.3 vs 263.9 (k=699); area at 29.333 ref 42435 vs ours 40296. Fix: delay the pull-out ease by about 0.4 frame, or shorten its ease-in.
11. **Push-in right edge bulges at 6.83–7.0.** Ref right edge at x=398, 431, 472, 460 (480) at k=164–168. Ours 404, 435, 475, 470. Left edge and top match to 1–3 px, so the zoom curve itself is right. Fix: trim the right-side growth by ~4 px at 480 in those frames, or move the ease tail ~1 frame earlier.
12. **Push-out 7.0–7.25 lags by ~1 frame.** Ref top edge 54 at 7.083 vs ours 58; at 7.125 ref 7 vs ours 11. At 7.25 ref has a sliver at x=141 that ours lacks. Fix: advance the tail of the push-out ease by ~1 frame.
13. **Pencil circles 6.3–6.9 too faint.** Ref has dense concentric circles plus a dotted inner ring at about 0.5 radius, with radial lines reaching the frame edge. Ours circles are at about 1/2 the alpha and lack the dotted ring. Fix: circle alpha ×1.5–2 (about #3A3A36 at 45%), add dotted inner ring at r≈0.5·R, lengthen radial lines to the frame edge.
14. **Arms-up pose at 30.8 s.** Ref right arm is angled about 20–25° up-right, left arm angled down-left. Ours right arm is closer to vertical. Fix: reduce the right-arm rotation by ~8–10°, compare against ref/f/0740 at the same arm pivot.
15. **Ground shadow under Clawd.** Ref has a grey pencil shadow ellipse under the feet (width about the body width, height ~6 px at 1920). Ours is present but lighter and narrower. Fix: widen to body width, darken to ~40% of the current alpha, and add the pencil hatch. Lowest priority.

Checks passed: Clawd eye/ground/body position at 1.0, 1.5, 2.2, 4.2, 4.8, 6.5, 29.3, 29.7, 30.8 (within 2–3 px at 1920); zoom curve 6.25–6.8 (within 1–2 px at 480); eye window at 28.87–29.2 (within 4 px at 480).
