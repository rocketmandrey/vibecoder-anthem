# Segment E critique (t = 21.25–28.75 s)

Measured with: `ref/tools/cmp.sh 21.25 28.75 E` (mean SSIM 0.792; worst 28.6–28.7 at 0.31–0.38), full-res side-by-sides, PIL sampling. Frame N = t·24+1 (ref 0511 = 21.25). Pixel coords are 1920×1080.

Timing is already close in most of the segment (per-second lag test: best lag = 0 for 22–26 s). The big errors are the flash, the beam/arc look, the Earth push, and the 27.6–28.3 island/dot section.

1. **Ink-burst flash holds too long (21.25–21.37, frames 511–513).** Ref: 0511 (21.25) black radial burst on cream (mean L 212); 0512 (21.29) is a near-white glow (centre #FFFAE7, mean L 250) with orange-gold rays (#F0B060); 0513 (21.33) is already the purple ring. Ours: 511 and 512 both show the black burst (mean L 222, identical frames), and 513 jumps straight to rings. Fix: make frame 512 a white glow with gold rays, not the black burst. Show the black burst on 511 only. Ring starts at 513.

2. **Earth push starts ~3 frames early and Earth is ~8–12% too wide (25.4–27.6).** Measured Earth top y (ref / ours): 25.42 → 681 / 672; 26.00 → 523 / 520; 26.50 → 440 / 443; 27.21 → **399 / 345**; 27.58 → **264 / 212**. Visible width at 27.21: 307 / 332 px. Ref push rate is ~365 px/s, same as ours, so the whole push is offset by ~54 px (~0.15 s). Fix: delay the push start by 3 frames (~0.13 s) and scale the Earth radius by ~0.92 during 25.4–27.2 (ref radius at 26.5 ≈ 150 px, ours ≈ 169 px). Recheck the top-y track after the fix.

3. **Missing white swirl burst before the island (27.58–27.8, frames 664–668).** Ref 0664 (27.62) has a bright white swirl blob (centre (960,570), diameter ~180 px, colour #F7F7FF) on the Earth's ocean. Ours at the same frame already shows the purple island at (945,540) with no white. Fix: add a white blob at (960,570), radius ~90 px, ramping from 27.58 to ~27.8, then reveal the island. Island should not appear before 27.8.

4. **Ocean colour and glow wrong during the push (27.8–28.0).** Ref ocean is flat #1C448A (sampled at (1000,300), (1400,300), (600,300) on 0669). Ours is #4F8CDD / #2B6DCD / #3C7FDA at the same points, with a cyan radial glow (#877EBF at the centre). Ref also shows thin dark concentric rings (#1F3F80, 1 px), not light ones. Fix: ocean base #1C448A, glow peak ≤10% opacity, ring strokes #1F3F80 at 1 px, rings drawn dark.

5. **Beam too thin and has no halo (22.45–23.75, frames 540–571).** At y=540, ref frame 0540 has a plateau of #FEFEFE about 45 px wide (x 940–985) and luminance 100–135 out to x≈900 (≈±60 px halo). Ours: a gaussian core of ~25 px (peak 241) and luminance 12 at x=900, so almost no halo. Ref spark head at 22.45 is a bright white disc radius ~28 px; ours is a dim ~12 px spot. Fix: beam core 44 px wide, flat #FFFFFF; glow gaussian sigma ~35 px peaking at ~#FFD27A, alpha ~0.6; spark head radius 28 px, bright white.

6. **Golden arc turns pale before it flattens (24.9–25.0, frames 599–601).** At 24.92 both arcs match (apex y 491 ref / 492 ours). By 25.00 the ref arc is still gold (median #E0B379, ~7,300 gold px, apex y 600 and flattening). Ours has only ~100 gold px and reads as cream/hairline. Fix: keep arc colour gold #E8B45A (lighter toward the tip, #F2C878) and ~6–8 px wide through 25.25. Flattening to the horizon line should happen at 25.0–25.25.

7. **Moon fill starts 2–3 frames late (25.33–25.4, frames 609–611).** At 25.33 ref already has a filled grey moon (#7E7E88 at (960,950)) with pencil-hatch texture, and the horizon edge sits at y≈615. Ours at 25.33 shows only the hairline horizon; the fill appears at ~25.4. Ours craters are bold dark ellipses, ref craters are soft outlined ellipses with lighter rims. Fix: fill the moon disc at frame 609, with hatch texture; craters as soft ellipses (~40×14 px at 1920, outline lighter than fill).

8. **White dot at centre arrives late and too small (28.25–28.6, frames 679–686).** Ref: white pixels (L>200) in a 50-px region at centre: 406 px on 0679 (28.25), 2,530 on 0681 (28.33), 3,190 on 0684 (28.46). Ours: 27 on 0679, 692 on 0681, 482 on 0684. Ref dot is ~28 px radius at 28.33, ours ~15 px. Fix: start the dot at frame 679 (28.25), radius 28 px at 681, grow to ~35 px by 686. Colour #FFFAE7 with a faint gold rim.

9. **Ring colour and centre disc wrong (21.5–21.8, frames 517–523).** Sampled along y=540: pink band ref #D44191 vs ours #DF52A1; dark band ref #41194B vs ours #54215A; centre disc at 21.75 ref #40223C (mauve, with white confetti sparkles across it), ours #1B0A36 (near-black with few streaks). Gold #E7B248 and cream #EDE3CA already match. Fix: pink #D44191, dark band #41194B, centre disc #40223C; add ~40–60 small white/gold sparkle dots across the disc at 21.7–21.85.

10. **Ring phase too dark (21.37–21.5, frames 514–517).** Mean luminance ref / ours: 514 → 89.6 / 79.7; 515 → 103.1 / 93.2; 516 → 112.8 / 106.0; 517 → 120.9 / 110.4. Ours is consistently ~10 L darker. Fix: brighter pink-white core glow (radius ~80 px, #FFE8F4 at centre) so the rings glow the same as ref.

11. **Warp field too uniform and disc too big (22.0–22.4, frames 529–539).** Ref at 22.0: inner dark disc spans ~510 px (of a 640-px tile, i.e. ~1530 px at 1920 scale); ours ~576 px (≈13% larger). Ref streaks are chunkier and multi-coloured (gold #E8B060, pink #E050A0, white) with sparkle dots at the ends; ours are thin, mostly white. Fix: shrink the disc radius by ~11%; use 2–3 px streaks with ~1/3 coloured (gold, pink) and add sparkle dots at the streak tips.

12. **Rocket flame and body differ (23.75–24.25, frames 571–583).** Ref flame is a narrow white-to-gold plume about the width of the beam core (~45 px at y540), with a dark panel gap at the rocket body (L 66 at x≈972). Ours has a wide orange dome (~60 px) at the base and a plain white body. Ref body is grey-panelled with a spiky white nose; ours is smooth. Fix: flame = beam-width plume (~45 px) with white core; body 40 px wide with grey panel lines (#B8BCC8) and a dark gap; nose with a small spiky tip.

13. **Trail too thick and glowing (24.25–24.6, frames 583–591).** Ref trail is ~10 px wide with thin gold core (#E0A850) and little glow; ours is ~15 px with a brighter halo at the rocket. Fix: trail width ~10 px, glow radius ~12 px at alpha 0.3, colour #E0A850. Keep the rocket end-point (ref ≈ (960,1280) at 24.6).

14. **Motion pulse mismatch (28.0–28.25, frames 673–679).** Ref frame-to-frame diff alternates (0.88, 0.47, 0.75, 0.52, 0.95, 0.53, 1.30, 0.55…), i.e. a pulsing zoom. Ours is smooth (0.62–0.70). Also the warp-to-Earth on-twos cadence (25.4–27.2) already matches ref (0.00 / ~3.4 alternating). Fix: add the alternating pulse to the zoom (about ±0.3 px per frame of the zoom term), no change to the cadence elsewhere.

15. **Soup bubble: size and position match; translucency does not (28.5–28.75, frames 685–691).** Ref ellipse spans x ≈ 540–1365 and fills to the frame top, ours matches within ~5%. Ref bubble is pale and translucent with a light rim; ours has a dark crescent on the right and a darker overall fill. Content is owned by another segment, so this only covers the bubble's shell. Fix: shell alpha ~0.6, rim #F4EEFF at 2 px, drop the dark right-hand crescent. Spiral at 28.75 (frame 691): ours has ~40 radial grey hatch strokes where ref has ~24; thin ours to match.
